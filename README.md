# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## About the Series

**Stranger Things** is a science-fiction and mystery series available on Netflix. The story follows a group of characters who encounter unusual events in the town of Hawkins.

## Why I Like It

I like this series because:

- The story is interesting and mysterious.
- The characters work together to solve problems.
- Every season introduces new challenges.
- The music and setting are memorable.

## Main Characters

1. Eleven
2. Mike Wheeler
3. Dustin Henderson
4. Lucas Sinclair
5. Will Byers
